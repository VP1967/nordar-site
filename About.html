◇ About Nordar, LLC

An independent technology firm for asset managers.

Nordar is an independent software consulting company that builds and supports the technology infrastructure behind financial asset management operations — designed, engineered, and operated as a single practice.

What we do.

We design and build technology infrastructure from the ground up for asset management firms — networking, server architecture, security, continuity, communications, back-office workflows, and the custom software that ties them together.

Most of our clients run hybrid environments: on-premise systems for performance-sensitive workloads, cloud where it makes sense. We deliver both with the same discipline.

How we work.

We engage as a vertically-integrated team. The engineers who scope an architecture build it, deploy it, and operate it through years of steady evolution. There is no handoff to a separate "managed services" group.

We pick problems we can do well. We say no to engagements where we can't be the right partner. The result is a small portfolio of long-running relationships rather than a long roster of short ones.

What guides the work.

Four commitments that shape every engagement, regardless of size or scope.

P.01

Built from the ground up.

We don't bolt onto fragile systems. Infrastructure is engineered as a coherent whole, with each layer serving the layers above it.

P.02

Reliability over novelty.

Asset management runs on uptime. We choose proven architectures and only deploy new technology when it earns its place.

P.03

Compliance as a baseline.

Security and regulatory posture are designed in, not bolted on after audit. Hardening, access, and monitoring are part of day-one architecture.

P.04

We stay with the work.

Continuous support is the default. The team that builds your infrastructure is the team that maintains it.
